Where to print these

Your method glues the print face-up and seals it with water-based Mod Podge, so toner beats inkjet — toner is fused plastic sitting on the paper and will not bleed when wet glue goes over it. Ask for a colour laser print at 100% scale on plain or lightweight paper. Thin paper actually conforms to the tile better than cardstock.
